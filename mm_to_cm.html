Millimeter

Definition: A millimeter (symbol: mm) is a unit of length in the metric system. One millimeter is equal to one-thousandth of a meter.

History/origin: The millimeter is based on the metric system and the meter. The metric system was developed in France during the late 18th century and uses decimal-based units.

Current use: Millimeters are commonly used for small measurements, technical drawings, engineering, manufacturing, component sizes, thicknesses, and precision measurements.

Centimeter

Definition: A centimeter (symbol: cm) is a unit of length in the metric system. One centimeter is equal to one-hundredth of a meter.

History/origin: The centimeter is part of the metric system. The prefix "centi" means one hundredth, making one centimeter equal to 0.01 meters.

Current use: Centimeters are widely used around the world for measuring height, width, small distances, clothing sizes, objects, and everyday measurements.

How to Convert Millimeter to Centimeter

1 mm = 0.1 cm

1 cm = 10 mm

Formula: centimeters = millimeters ÷ 10

Example: Convert 120 mm to centimeters:
120 ÷ 10 = 12 cm
