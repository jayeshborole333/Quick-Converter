Inch

Definition: An inch (symbol: in) is a unit of length in the imperial and US customary systems, equal to 1/12 of a foot or 2.54 centimeters.

History/origin: The inch has historically been associated with the width of a human thumb. It was standardized internationally to exactly 2.54 cm.

Current use: Inches are commonly used in the United States and the United Kingdom for small measurements, screen sizes, paper sizes, and other applications.

Yard

Definition: A yard (symbol: yd) is a unit of length equal to 36 inches or exactly 0.9144 meters.

Current use: Yards are commonly used for field-length measurements in sports and for various everyday measurements in the United States and United Kingdom.
