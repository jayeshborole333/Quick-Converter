CONVERSION FORMULA

Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit Formula

Fahrenheit:

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Celsius:

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

EXAMPLE

How to Convert 15°C to Fahrenheit

Use the Celsius to Fahrenheit formula:

15 × 9/5 + 32 = 59°F

Therefore, 15°C = 59°F.
