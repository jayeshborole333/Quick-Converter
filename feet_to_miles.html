Foot

Definition: A foot (symbol: ft) is a unit of length in the imperial and US customary systems. One foot equals 12 inches or exactly 0.3048 meters.

History/origin: The foot was historically based on the length of the human foot. It was standardized internationally in 1959 as exactly 0.3048 meters.

Current use: Feet are commonly used in the United States and the United Kingdom for height, elevation, construction, and shorter distances.

Mile

Definition: A mile (symbol: mi) is a unit of length equal to 5,280 feet or exactly 1,609.344 meters.

History/origin: The word mile comes from the Roman term "mille passus", meaning one thousand paces.

Current use: Miles are commonly used in the United States and United Kingdom for road distances, travel distances, and geographical measurements.
