Centimeter

Definition: A centimeter (symbol: cm) is a unit of length in the International System of Units (SI). It is equal to one hundredth of a meter.

History/origin: The centimeter is based on the SI unit meter, with the prefix "centi" indicating one hundredth.

Current use: Centimeters are widely used worldwide to measure height, small objects, distances, and everyday measurements.

Inch

Definition: An inch (symbol: in) is a unit of length in the imperial and US customary systems. One inch is exactly equal to 25.4 millimeters.

History/origin: The word "inch" comes from the Latin "uncia", meaning one-twelfth of a Roman foot.

Current use: Inches are commonly used in the US, UK, and Canada, especially for height, screen sizes, and construction.
